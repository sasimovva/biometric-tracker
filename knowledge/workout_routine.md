# Precision Training Protocol: Aerobic Base & Muscle Preservation

This training program integrates High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) on the indoor bike and Versaclimber with low-intensity Zone 2 conditioning, hiking, and rucking. It is designed to maximize visceral fat loss, protect the endothelial lining (under 25mg Losartan), and preserve skeletal muscle mass (Target: 69.2 lbs).

## ⚙️ Modality Execution Guides

### 1. HIIT (Versaclimber / Indoor Bike)
* **Warm-up:** 5 minutes easy spinning / climbing.
* **Format options:**
  * **Versaclimber:** 20 sets of `30s work / 30s active rest` (focus on steady, paced output; do not push to absolute failure).
  * **Indoor Bike:** 10 sets of `1 min high-cadence sprint / 1 min easy spin` (great for legs and blood flow).
* **Cool-down:** 3–5 minutes easy spinning/stretching.

### 2. Rucking (Weighted Hikes)
* **Gear:** Use a dedicated rucking backpack or standard pack with weights positioned high and tight against your back.
* **Loading Guide:**
  * *Week 1–2:* Start with **15 lbs** (approx. 20% of skeletal muscle mass baseline).
  * *Week 3–4:* Progress to **20 lbs** if shoulders and lower back feel stable.
* **Postural Target:** Keep shoulders retracted and chest open. This opens up your lungs, preventing the shallow breathing common when carrying a heavy load.

### 3. Outdoor Hikes (Long-Distance)
* **Goal:** Sustained, steady-state climbing. Keep your heart rate below your ventilatory threshold (Zone 2) to optimize fat oxidation and prevent central nervous system fatigue.

## 🚨 Safety & Progression Guardrails

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Paced HIIT Progression:**
> Because you are re-introducing HIIT, focus strictly on **pacing**. Do not push to absolute breathlessness (Zone 5) in the first 2 weeks. Aim for Zone 4 peak heart rates. This ensures endothelial lining protection and prevents sudden BP spikes.

> [!TIP]
> **Hydration Matching:**
> Increase your fluid intake by **500ml** on Saturday (Long Hike) and Sunday (Rucking) days to support the higher physical volume and creatine processing.
