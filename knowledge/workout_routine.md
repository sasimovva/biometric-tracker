# Precision Training Protocol: Aerobic Base & Muscle Preservation

This training program integrates High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) on the indoor bike and Versaclimber with low-intensity Zone 2 conditioning, hiking, and rucking. It is designed to maximize visceral fat loss, protect the endothelial lining (under 25mg Losartan), and preserve skeletal muscle mass (Target: 69.2 lbs).

## ⚙️ Modality Execution Guides

### 1. HIIT (Versaclimber Only)
* **Warm-up:** 5 minutes easy climbing.
* **Format:** 20 sets of `30s work / 30s active rest` (focus on steady, paced output; do not push to absolute failure).
* **Cool-down:** 3–5 minutes easy climbing/stretching.

### 2. parkrun 5K (Saturday Timed Run)
* **Pacing Guide:** Since this is a timed event, start at a controlled, sustainable jog (Zone 3). Focus on a consistent tempo rather than sprinting at the start.
* **Objective:** Build sustained high-aerobic capacity without triggering excessive systemic fatigue.
* **Warm-up:** 5 minutes of dynamic stretching and easy walking prior to start.

### 3. Rucking (Sunday Weighted Hikes)
* **Gear:** Use a dedicated rucking backpack or standard pack with weights positioned high and tight against your back.
* **Loading Guide:**
  * *Week 1–2:* Start with **15 lbs** (approx. 20% of skeletal muscle mass baseline).
  * *Week 3–4:* Progress to **20 lbs** if shoulders and lower back feel stable.
* **Postural Target:** Keep shoulders retracted and chest open to prevent shallow breathing.

### 4. Active Recovery & Zone 2 (Walking / Hiking)
* **Target:** Steady, continuous movement on flat or gently rolling terrain. 
* **Rule:** If you cannot carry on a full conversation without pausing for breath, you have slipped out of Zone 2. Slow down your pace to maximize fat oxidation.

## 🚨 Safety & Progression Guardrails

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **HIIT & Running Progression:**
> Because you are re-introducing intervals and running, focus strictly on **pacing**. Do not push to absolute breathlessness (Zone 5) in the first 2 weeks. Aim for Zone 4 peak heart rates. This ensures endothelial lining protection and prevents sudden BP spikes.

> [!TIP]
> **Hydration Matching:**
> Increase your fluid intake by **500ml** on Saturday (parkrun) and Sunday (Rucking) days to support the higher physical volume and creatine processing.
