Because your gut microbiome is fully conditioned to handle 40g of RS2 and 5g of inulin, your bacteria will ferment these heavy substrates into short-chain fatty acids (specifically butyrate and propionate) all evening. These SCFAs continuously stimulate the L-cells in your distal colon, generating a natural, ongoing release of GLP-1 during your 18-hour fast. This hormonal signaling completely eliminates evening food cravings.

### 🔋 3. Dual-Staking Creatine and Gut Support

Simultaneously, providing a daily 5g dose of creatine supplies direct energetic support (ATP) to your intestinal epithelial cells, helping to reinforce your tight junctions and maintain a strong gut barrier.

### 🚶 4. Maximizing the 18-Hour Fast with Walking
Walking keeps your stress hormones (cortisol) completely low while operating entirely within your aerobic fat-oxidation window. To optimize this setup, **do your 3-mile walk in the morning before your 9:00 AM breakfast.** Walking at the end of your 18-hour fast—when your insulin levels are at an absolute baseline—forces your body to immediately mobilize stored fatty acids, directly targeting the highly vascular visceral fat surrounding your organs.
